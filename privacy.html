Privacy Policy

Layerix Studio · Effective 5 October 2026

In short: Layerix Studio does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your designs and files stay on your device.

Who we are

Layerix Studio is a design editor for Windows developed and published by Prof. Hani Muhsen ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Layerix Studio does not ask you to create an account, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not track how you use the app.

Your files

  • Files you open (PSD, PDF, SVG, images and Layerix projects) are read and processed only on your own device.
  • Files are saved only where you choose in the Windows Save dialog. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • When you copy and paste, the app uses your device's clipboard only for the content you copy.

Information stored on your device

To make the app more convenient, a few items are kept in the app's local storage on your PC and never leave it:

  • Your preferences (theme, brush and text settings, recent colours, export settings).
  • The list of recently opened files, with small preview images, so you can reopen them. You can clear it at any time from the start screen.
  • Fonts you choose to add to the app.

Uninstalling Layerix Studio removes this information.

Permissions

  • Files: the app can only open or save a file after you pick it in a Windows file dialog, drop it into the window or open it with Layerix Studio from File Explorer.
  • Installed fonts (optional): if you choose "Show all fonts installed on this PC", Windows asks for permission to share the list of font names with the app. The list is used only to show fonts in the font menu.

Internet connection

The app is installed through the Microsoft Store and its program files are hosted on GitHub Pages. After the first start it works offline. When an internet connection is available, the app may download a newer version of its own program files. These requests never contain your designs or files. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the files; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Layerix Studio does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
